Initial release of ClearNet FOSS
- Website blocking: local VPN DNS filter with domain, keyword and allow lists.
- Browser checks that keep working with another VPN on.
- App blocking.
- SafeSearch for Google, Bing and YouTube.
- Uninstall protection via Device Admin and PIN.
- App lock with PIN reset, settings lock and disguise.
- Disable confirmation challenge.
- Blocklist backup/restore and custom DNS.
- Stats and themes.
- No accounts, trackers or ads.
