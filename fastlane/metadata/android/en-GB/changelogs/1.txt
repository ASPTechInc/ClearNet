Initial release of ClearNet FOSS!
- Secure parental app control built with Flutter.
- Uninstall protection via safe Device Admin rules.
- Offline custom website blocklist filtering.
- Local VPNService intercepts traffic completely offline.
- SafeSearch enforcement for Google, Bing, and YouTube.
- In-app Changelog, Source Code, and Support links.
- Privacy-focused: no remote cloud accounts, trackers, or ads.
